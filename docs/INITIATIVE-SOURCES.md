# Initiative provenance

Checked 5 October 2026. Dates below distinguish launch from campaign proposal and legislative foundation. A proposal for the 2024 election can appear in a manifesto published in 2023. All programs are available in the hypothetical game from the start; availability is not a historical implementation claim. Costs and effects are game assumptions.

| Initiative | Attribution and primary evidence |
|---|---|
| MBG | Prabowo, national rollout January 2025. [BGN retrospective](https://www.bgn.go.id/news/berita/presiden-instruksikan-percepatan-program-mbg-pemerintah-siapkan-perpres-khusus) confirms 6 January. The originally supplied BGN launch article intermittently rejected the research fetch; the catalog retains that source link. |
| Kopdes Merah Putih | Prabowo, 2025. [Kemenkop release, 21 July 2025](https://kop.go.id/read/presiden-prabowo-resmikan-kopdes-kel-merah-putih-momentum-kembalikan-sistem-ekonomi-ke-pasal-33-uud-45) describes institutional inauguration and subsequent operational preparation, not universal full operation on that date. |
| CKG | Prabowo, 2025. [Kemenkes announcement](https://www.kemkes.go.id/id/cek-kesehatan-gratis-kado-ulang-tahun-dimulai-10-februari-2025) and [BKPK launch report](https://www.badankebijakan.kemkes.go.id/cek-kesehatan-gratis-dimulai-serentak/) identify 10 February. |
| Badan Penerimaan Negara | Prabowo–Gibran, 2024 election proposal. [KPU manifesto, PDF page 2, quick-result item 8](https://www.kpu.go.id/dmdocument/1704254649Lampiran_Tim_Kampanye_Paslon_2_01-12-2023T22.02.39.pdf). The advertised revenue target is deliberately not a game guarantee. |
| PKH | Began under SBY in 2007. [Cabinet Secretariat contemporary account](https://setkab.go.id/en/dambaan-rakyat-menuju-sejahtera/) identifies first launch in 2007 and later budget expansion. The supplied Kemensos guide was intermittently inaccessible; its source link remains in the catalog. |
| BOS | Began under SBY, July 2005. [Education ministry history](https://jendela.kemendikdasmen.go.id/v2/berita/detail/sejarah-dan-peran-bos-bagi-pendidikan-indonesia) distinguishes launch, 2011 decentralization and 2020 direct transfers. |
| KUR | SBY, 2007. [Cabinet Secretariat, July 2014](https://setkab.go.id/en/kur-meningkatan-pendapatan-umkm-di-boyolali-2/) describes implementation since 2007. Simulated support is a business-investment proxy, not a bank loan book. |
| JKN | Launched under SBY, 1 January 2014; spans administrations. [Kemenkes launch release](https://www.kemkes.go.id/eng/presiden-luncurkan-bpjs-dan-jkn). SJSN legislation predates the launch; subsequent expansion is not a new invention. |
| Dana Desa | First allocations under Jokowi, 2015. [Treasury's ten-year history](https://djpb.kemenkeu.go.id/kppn/balikpapan/id/data-publikasi/artikel/3055-10-tahun-dana-desa.html) identifies allocations beginning in 2015. The 2014 Village Law predates those allocations. |
| Tol Laut | Jokowi, November 2015. [Transport Ministry launch release](https://dephub.go.id/post/read/program-tol-laut-resmi-diluncurkan) identifies 4 November. |
| Prakerja | Jokowi, 2020. [Coordinating Economy Ministry first-registration release](https://ekon.go.id/publikasi/detail/226/pemerintah-resmi-buka-pendaftaran-kartu-prakerja-tahap-pertama); [later ministry account](https://ekon.go.id/publikasi/detail/528/gelombang-10-kartu-prakerja-dibuka-kuota-tahun-anggaran-2020-terpenuhi) confirms registration opened 11 April 2020. |
| Integrated public transport | Descriptive label for an Anies–Muhaimin 2024 election proposal. [Supplied AMIN manifesto](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1BOfwwq9NHMwiGJIaeDOCoko5Y9HRwnkQ/view) was downloaded and text-extracted: the urban-development section proposes mass rail/road transport integrated with active mobility and zero-emission vehicles. The Sulawesi section also proposes integrated public transport. This is a national proposal, distinct from Anies's previous Jakarta policies. |
| Pupuk dan Benih Murah | Anies–Muhaimin, 2024 election proposal. [KPU manifesto, PDF page 2, item 16](https://www.kpu.go.id/dmdocument/1704254632Lampiran_Tim_Kampanye_Paslon_1_01-12-2023T22.02.35.pdf) names affordable and available fertilizer/seeds. |
| Village clinic and health worker | Ganjar–Mahfud, 2024 election proposal. [KPU manifesto, PDF page 1, item 1.1.1](https://www.kpu.go.id/dmdocument/1704254668Lampiran_Tim_Kampanye_Paslon_3_01-12-2023T22.02.40.pdf). The game does not claim every village received these facilities. |
| 1 Keluarga Miskin, 1 Sarjana | Ganjar–Mahfud, 2024 election proposal. [KPU manifesto, PDF page 2, item 1.2.2](https://www.kpu.go.id/dmdocument/1704254668Lampiran_Tim_Kampanye_Paslon_3_01-12-2023T22.02.40.pdf). |

The game uses the catalog's bilingual historical notes and source links. Source/sector/status filters expose provenance independently from simulated performance. Ministries, parties and cabinet members in the simulation remain fictional.
