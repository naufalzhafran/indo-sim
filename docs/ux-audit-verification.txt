  Slot and launch limits show associated explanations.

  failed-vote and validation messages appear beside Advance quarter.
- Quarter progression, final outcome, saving, import/export, reload, recap,
  replay, reduced motion and WebGL context loss pass existing regression cases.
- Region selection reveals and focuses details; priority buttons and all three
  distribution rules update the plan; the three-priority limit is explained.
- Political answers update the navigation badge, vote meter and forecast.

- GameSelect and StatHelp keyboard, focus, containment and Escape behavior pass.
- New presidency returns to setup. Forecast hint opens and dismisses with Escape.

Design reasons
- Palette: existing cream/teal/emerald/coral tokens preserve the approved game.
- Layout: pin consequences above the turn action; scroll only the editable plan.
- Typography: retain Nunito controls and Source Sans 3 for dense numeric content.
- Spacing: compact repeated headings to expose a complete program at 1280x720.
- Cards: slots express capacity; selected-region details explain the map click.
- Markers: signed arrows and vote/priority chips expose actual state, without
  relying on color alone. Spending changes are neutral rather than judged good.
- Art: the archipelago remains the background focus; this work adds no scenery.
- Design read: desktop island-game decisions, ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.
